Design Principles

Good Web Design

Why Design Matters

Good design helps users understand information quickly and complete tasks with less confusion. A well-designed page uses readable typography, strong contrast, clear spacing, and a consistent layout.

Design Tip

Keep your page simple, readable, and focused on the user's needs. White space is not wasted space; it helps guide attention.

Important Principles

  • Typography
  • Color contrast
  • Clarity
  • Consistency
  • Visual hierarchy
  • Accessibility
Design for the user, not just for appearance.
